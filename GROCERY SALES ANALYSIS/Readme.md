This is a Grocery Sales Dashboard created to analyze overall sales performance. It shows important KPIs such as total sales, orders, quantity and unit price. The dashboard also analyzes sales trends, regional sales, top-selling categories and payment modes. The second page provides detailed product-level analysis, geographical sales distribution and customer segmentation. Filters such as year, quarter, month, region, city and payment mode allow users to interact with the dashboard and analyze the data based on their requirements.
